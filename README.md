# Colonial Electric — homepage design concept

A redesign concept for [colonialelec.com](https://www.colonialelec.com), Colonial Electric Co. (Lothian, MD), shared for review. It is not their live site: the page carries a banner saying so, is set to `noindex`, and the contact form is not connected.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | The page (static HTML). |
| `colonial.css` | Styles. Colours and fonts are CSS variables at the top. Bump `colonial.css?v=N` in `index.html` when it changes. |

Content is written from facts on their current site. Images are their own logo and building photos, loaded from their Wix media library. Two AI-generated images and a "Batman signal" graphic on the current site were deliberately left out.

## If they go ahead

1. Replace the building-type captions with real project names, locations and scope.
2. Add license numbers, reviews and any real photos of service and underground work.
3. Connect the form (Web3Forms key for their inbox) and remove the preview banner and `noindex`.
