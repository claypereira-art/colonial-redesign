Our family

Three generations of Katskis

Colonial Electric was started by a father and son, kept alive by a husband and wife working nights, and is run today by their son. The family's electrical work goes back even further than the company.

  1. 1951

    Clayton Katski

    Starts his first electrical firm, Bay Electric, from his home in Tracey's Landing, and helps form Associated Builders and Contractors, the merit-shop association Colonial is still active in.

  2. 1970

    Butch & Jo Ann Katski

    Robert "Butch" Katski talks his father into going out on their own. After signing their first contract in November 1969, Colonial Electric becomes official on January 29, 1970. Butch and Jo Ann work nights to keep it going, and in the early 1980s they move into commercial and multifamily work.

  3. 2000

    Travis Katski

    Their son, already working at Colonial, takes over. Today he is Executive Chairman, Jo Ann is still Executive Vice President, and Aaron Wood leads the company as President.
